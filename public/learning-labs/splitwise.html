      Split the trip and confirm two payments

      1. Before the trip. Positive means is owed; negative means owes. All amounts are INR paise. A: INR 0.00; B: INR 0.00; C: INR 0.00.
      2. A pays INR 100.01 for three people. Shares are A 3334, B 3334, C 3333 paise. A's own share cancels inside A's balance. A: INR 66.67; B: INR -33.34; C: INR -33.33.
      3. B pays INR 30 for B and C. B is owed 1500 more; C owes 1500 more. The expense does not change A's balance. A: INR 66.67; B: INR -18.34; C: INR -48.33.
      4. B reports a payment of INR 18.34. A has not confirmed receipt. The proposal does not reduce anyone's balance. A: INR 66.67; B: INR -18.34; C: INR -48.33.
      5. A confirms B's payment. B's balance becomes zero. A's remaining credit equals C's remaining debt. A: INR 48.33; B: INR 0.00; C: INR -48.33.
      6. A confirms C's INR 48.33. All three balances are zero; the two expenses remain in the journal. A: INR 0.00; B: INR 0.00; C: INR 0.00.

      Retries and a suggestion that becomes stale

      1. Before recording the taxi. No expense or payment is recorded yet. A: INR 0.00; B: INR 0.00; C: INR 0.00.
      2. Original taxi expense. There is one expense entry. A: INR 66.67; B: INR -33.34; C: INR -33.33.
      3. Same expense identity, same data. The retry returns the original expense. It adds no second entry. A: INR 66.67; B: INR -33.34; C: INR -33.33.
      4. Same identity, changed amount is rejected. The prior expense and balances are preserved. A: INR 66.67; B: INR -33.34; C: INR -33.33.
      5. B reports INR 33.34 from the old suggestion. A confirmation is still pending; a suggestion reserves nothing. A: INR 66.67; B: INR -33.34; C: INR -33.33.
      6. Meanwhile B pays INR 12 for A. B now owes only INR 21.34, but the reported transfer amount remains INR 33.34. A: INR 54.67; B: INR -21.34; C: INR -33.33.
      7. A confirms the actual INR 33.34 received. Record the actual confirmed payment once. B now has INR 12 credit; do not rewrite what was received to fit today's debt. A: INR 21.33; B: INR 12.00; C: INR -33.33.
      8. A retries confirmation. The confirmed payment adds no second journal entry. A: INR 21.33; B: INR 12.00; C: INR -33.33.

      Correct an expense after money was received

      1. Before recording dinner. No expense or payment is recorded yet. A: INR 0.00; B: INR 0.00; C: INR 0.00.
      2. A records dinner for A and B. B owes A INR 45. A: INR 45.00; B: INR -45.00; C: INR 0.00.
      3. B's payment is confirmed. The balances are zero before the correction. A: INR 0.00; B: INR 0.00; C: INR 0.00.
      4. The dinner expense was a mistake. Append its opposite entry. A now owes B INR 45 because the cash already received was not undone. A: INR -45.00; B: INR 45.00; C: INR 0.00.
      5. Retry the same correction. There is still only one reversal. A suggested refund is advice, not an automatic bank transfer. A: INR -45.00; B: INR 45.00; C: INR 0.00.

      Nothing plays automatically. Every frame is a saved result of SplitLedger; amounts are stored as integer paise and displayed here as rupees.
