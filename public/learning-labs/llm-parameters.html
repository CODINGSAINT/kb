Read the worked comparison

At T=1 the probabilities are 0.5, 0.25, 0.15, 0.1. Top-p=0.7 retains the first two: mass 0.75, renormalized 2/3 and 1/3. At T=0.5 they become approximately 0.724638, 0.181159, 0.065217, 0.028986; the same top-p retains only the first. Lower temperature can sharpen a wrong leader just as easily.
